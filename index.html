Public Sector · $12M ACV

Turning a stalled municipal RFP into a signed multi year contract

Queen Toba Investment, Dubai
Situation

A regional government account had cycled through two vendors in 18 months. Procurement was skeptical, the technical evaluator was blocking, and the deal had been sitting at 40% probability for a full quarter.

Approach

Ran a fresh discovery loop with each of the three decision makers separately, mapped their conflicting success criteria, and rebuilt the proposal around a phased rollout that lowered the risk in year one. Brought in a solutions lead for a joint working session to turn the technical blocker into a champion.

Outcome

Closed a three year contract 6 weeks ahead of the revised forecast, with an expansion clause for two adjacent departments already scoped.

IoT and SaaS · US Market Entry

Breaking into the US land surveying market from a cold start

Zenatech, Florida territory
Situation

Zenatech is a SaaS and DaaS company expanding Drone as a Service across North America, and had recently acquired several land surveying firms in the US. Existing outbound scripts were built for a technical buyer and converted under 1% to meetings in the new segment. I was assigned the Florida territory with no warm pipeline.

Approach

Instead of mass calling, I rebuilt pipeline management around real relationships: mapping each target account to the right people, running disciplined multi touch follow ups, and coaching our operations team through vendor approval so we could be considered for real projects.

Outcome

Got Zenatech onto multiple Florida vendor lists and turned approvals into live project work, making a cold territory a repeatable source of qualified US opportunities.
